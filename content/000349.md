One of my few memories from elementary school
was writing a terminal game to play with myself in Python 2.
I didn't know `time.sleep` existed, so I had homebaked my own to the tune of

```python
for _ in range(C * num_seconds):
    pass
```

with C worked out empirically.
Back then I had two-space indents,
and my editor was [DrPython](https://drpython.sourceforge.net/index.html).

I kinda wish I could see the old programs just to laugh at it,
but I don't know if I still have that ancient laptop.
