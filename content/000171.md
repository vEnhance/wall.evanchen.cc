[EWD 854][854] and [EWD 1036][1036] talk about the dangers of trying to make
predictions about the future by using analogies to the past. Actually I feel
like I haven't heard many other people say the same thing.

What happens if I tune out all predictions about radically new territory which
are justified with a comparison to the past? Is that ignoring history?
