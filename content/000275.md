A story in amateur form design.

It used to be that on the OTIS financial aid form,
people didn't know the difference between annual vs monthly income.
(No matter which one I asked for, some students would put the other.)

Solution: ask for _both_ annual vs monthly income,
and have JavaScript validate that `monthly ≈ annual ÷ 12`.
