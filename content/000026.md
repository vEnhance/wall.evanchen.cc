Today was another "oh crap the kernel broke time to regenerate `fstab`" day.
My laptop's `/boot` ran out of space.

Turns out there isn't actually any reason
that `initramfs` or `vmlinuz-linux` need to go on the EFI partition.
So the solution was to move the EFI stuff onto a new `/efi` directory,
and change `/boot` from a mount point to just another folder on root partition.
[Thanks Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/archlinux/comments/sdtp27/comment/huf0dia/).
