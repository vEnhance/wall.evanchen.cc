I finally put [two and two together](/228)
and realized that `size(8cm);` is actually `size(8*cm);` in Asymptote.
That is, `cm` is actually just a real number:

```text
Welcome to Asymptote version 3.13 (to view the manual, type help)
> cm
28.3464566929134
```
