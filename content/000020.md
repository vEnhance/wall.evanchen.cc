One thing that's always surprised me about things like contest math or chess or
StarCraft is how people can express a personality through their play style. For
something formalizable with a clearly definede objective function, it's still
not clear to me _a priori_ that should happen.

Maybe that's a symptom of depth to look for in domains --- whether it naturally
lets people express unique personalities.
