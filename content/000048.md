I kind of think "irregular conjugation" was a poor choice of name for the verb
conjugations that follow one of several rules depending on the ending, because
they're still mostly following a fixed rule.

To me "irregular" should be edge cases like 돕다 becoming 도와 which are
_actually_ edge cases
([irregular irregulars](https://blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=timewithsiha&logNo=223647715875), anyone?).
