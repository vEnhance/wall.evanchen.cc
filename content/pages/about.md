Hi friend.
If you don't know me, my name is [Evan][evan].
I'm human, but my life aspiration is to be a cat.
I'm not in school anymore, and I don't have an office job either.
So I spend a lot of time at home myself now
[tinkering with my toys][dotfiles] and so on.

I already have a [blog that a few people read][blog],
but I feel like everything I post on that blog must be a polished essay.
So sometimes I just want a [wall I can talk to](https://w.wiki/F3QV).
Kind of like something I can scribble a sentence in at a time.

Actually that's kind of what Twitter was supposed to be,
but every time I open `x.com`, I die a little inside.
So one day I decided I'd just build my own thing, and now you're reading it.

I hope you enjoy the [writing on the wall](https://w.wiki/F3QX).
